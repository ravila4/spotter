# General activity

The [HHS adult guidelines](../sources/hhs-2018-physical-activity-guidelines-2ed.md)
provide a population-health reference: 150–300 minutes of moderate aerobic
activity, or 75–150 minutes of vigorous activity, or an equivalent mixture per
week, plus strengthening on at least two days. Benefits can occur below these
amounts. These are not sport-performance targets.

## What steps tell us

Daily steps describe movement over time. They include light activity, so a step
total alone cannot establish moderate or vigorous minutes. This source does not
make 10,000 daily steps a guideline or provide a universal steps-to-minutes
conversion. The source note links the relevant pages.

## A possible weekly summary

| Component | What to report | What remains unknown |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Steps | Dated daily totals and available trend | Intensity of those steps |
| Aerobic activity | Minutes whose intensity is known | Unclassified minutes and missing sessions |
| Strength work | Known days and recorded exercises | Whether all major muscle groups and sufficient effort were covered |

An approximate moderate-equivalent total is `moderate_minutes + 2 ×
vigorous_minutes`, as described in the guideline. This is health-guideline
accounting, not a session-RPE score. Imported and manually logged versions of
the same activity should count once. A missing record cannot establish
inactivity.

The guideline's talk test can help describe relative effort conversationally;
it is not automatically a Garmin zone mapping. See [running intensity](running.md)
and [the schema mapping](using-with-spotter.md) before planning imports or reports.
